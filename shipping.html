Known Caveats

1. Excel-mangled AWBs: Delhivery AWB numbers in source CSVs arrive as scientific notation (4.05944E+13) — unusable for tracking joins. Fix at source: upload exports without opening in Excel.
2. Coverage gaps: file exports are date-windowed; orders outside the window keep their last-known status.
3. COD remittance: field captured only from June 2026 files onward.
4. Zone field: blank in most panel exports — zone analysis only covers rows where the courier returned it.
